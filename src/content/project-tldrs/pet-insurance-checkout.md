**Problem:** The review page is the last screen before someone enters their payment details. Most Perfect Pet sales come through comparison sites, so it's often the first time the buyer has come across the brand at all. The Hotjar recordings showed people spending five minutes or more on the page, opening policy PDFs to work out what they were buying, and a fair few leaving to check the main site and never coming back.

**Approach:** I did a competitor review, watched Hotjar recordings of the live page, and interviewed 11 colleagues from around the business. I only recruited internally because the timeline forced me to, and I expected it to be a weak spot. It wasn't. These are the people who deal with the complaints and declined claims, so they knew exactly what happens when a page makes the cover sound better than it is.

**Constraints:** One two-week sprint. Everything had to be built with MudBlazor components, and the policy wording had to stay word for word for FCA compliance. I couldn't change what the page said, only how it was laid out.

**Outcome:** I reworked the page so the details people need at that point come first and the wall of policy text comes later. Pre-existing conditions got their own page, and the excess moved up to where people could see it. The redesign is live now, and thousands of pet insurance customers go through it every week.
